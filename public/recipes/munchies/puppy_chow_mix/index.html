Puppy Chow Mix

Munchies, Peanut Butter, Chocolate Chips, Powdered Sugar, Chex Cereal, Butter,

Ingredients

  1. Peanut Butter — 3/4 cup
  2. Chocolate Chips — 1 cup
  3. Butter — 1/4 cup
  4. Chex Cereal — 8 cups
  5. Powdered Sugar
  6. large brown paper bag

Cookware

  1. medium saucepan
  2. large bowl

Instructions

  1. Melt the Peanut Butter, Chocolate Chips, and Butter in a medium saucepan over low heat, stirring constantly until smooth.

  2. Once melted pour over the Chex Cereal in a large bowl and stir until the cereal is evenly coated.

  3. Then pour Powdered Sugar into a large brown paper bag and add the cereal mixture. Seal the bag and shake until the cereal is evenly coated with powdered sugar.
