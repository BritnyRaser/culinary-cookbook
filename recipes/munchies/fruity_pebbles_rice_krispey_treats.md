---
Title: Fruity Pebbles Rice Krispy Treats
Tags: Munchies, Homemade
---

Take a #9 x 13 inch pan{} and grease it with @butter. 

In a #large saucepan{}, melt @butter{1/4 cup} over low heat. 

Add @marshmallows{1 package or 45 marshmellows} and stir until completely melted. 

Remove from heat and add @Rice Krispies cereal{3 cups} & @Fruity Pebbles{3 cups}. 

Stir until well coated. 

Press the mixture evenly into the prepared pan using a buttered spatula or wax paper. 

Let cool for about 30 minutes, then cut into squares and serve. Enjoy your homemade Rice Krispie Treats!
