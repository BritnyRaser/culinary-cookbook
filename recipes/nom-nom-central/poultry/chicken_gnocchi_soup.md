---
Title: Chicken Gnocchi Soup
Tags: Nom Nom Central, Poultry, Soup
---

In #large pot{}, heat @Oil{1 Tablespoon} over medium heat; add @Onion{1/2}, @Garlic{3 Cloves}, diced @Chicken{4 breasts}, and shredded @Carrots{1/2 cup}.

Saute for 3 minute until cooked.

Add @Chicken Broth{4 cups}, @Salt, @Black Pepper{}, @Thyme{1/2 teaspoon}.  Bring to a boil, then gently stir in @Gnocchi{16 oz}.

Boil for 4 minutes before reducing heat to a simmer and cook for 10 minutes.

Stir in @Heavy Cream{2 cups}, @Spinach{1 cup}, & @Parmesan Cheese{1 cup}. 

Cook for 2 minutes until Spinach is tender.  Season as needed.
