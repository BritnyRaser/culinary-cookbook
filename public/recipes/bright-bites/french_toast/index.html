French Toast

American, Bright Bites, Bread

Ingredients

  1. Eggs — 3-4
  2. Milk
  3. Bread Slices — 4
  4. Butter
  5. Maple Syrup
  6. Butter
  7. Powdered Sugar

Cookware

  1. plate
  2. bowl
  3. pan

Instructions

  1. Crack Eggs onto a plate or bowl

  2. Add Milk and stir into the eggs

  3. Take Bread Slices and place into the egg mixture to coat both sides of the bread

  4. Heat a pan with Butter coated on it

  5. Place the bread slices into the pan

  6. Flip when needed until both sides are golden brown

  7. Serve with Maple Syrup (Butter and Powdered Sugar are optional)
