---
Title: Mama's Soup
Tags: Beef, Poultry, Chicken, Homemade
---

Place @Beef Roast{1.5 lbs} or @Chicken Breasts{4}, @Beef Cubes{3} or @Chicken Cubes{3}, @Bay Leaf{1}, @All Spice{3 whole}, @Salt, @Black Pepper{}, & @Garlic{2 teaspoons} in an #Instant Pot{}.

Add vegetables such as @Carrots{} & @Potatoes{}.

Pour in enough @Water to cover the meat.

Cook on high pressure for about ~{45 minutes}.

Meanwhile, combine @Eggs{3} & @Flour{2 cups} to make the noodles.

Roll the dough out & cut it into thin, short pieces.

Remove the meat from the Instant Pot & shred it; set it aside.

Bring the broth to a boil & drop in the noodles.

Once the noodles are cooked through, return the shredded meat to the pot & serve.
