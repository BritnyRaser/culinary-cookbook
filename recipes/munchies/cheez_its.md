---
Title: Cheez-Its
Servings: 1
Link: https://nobunplease.com/my-crispy-cheddar-crisps/
---

Preheat oven to 250°F.

Cut @Cheddar Cheese{} into quarters & spread out on a parchment-lined baking sheet (about an inch apart)
(Optional) To resemble Cheez-its, you can add a hole to the center of each using a straw 

Sprinkle with salt.

Put into oven & bake for roughly ~{35%minutes}. You want to to look for a golden brown color.

Remove from oven & let cool for 10-15 minutes.
