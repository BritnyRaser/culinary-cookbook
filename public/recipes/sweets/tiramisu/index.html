Directions

  1. Separate the Egg Yolks from the Egg Whites and place the Egg Whites in the Kitchen Aid bowl and Egg Yolks into a separate flat container.

  2. Add Sugar to the bowl with the Egg Whites and beat on high until stiff peaks form about map[index:0 type:timer]. Set aside in another bowl.

  3. Pour the Sugar into the bowl with the Egg Yolks and beat them on high unti the mixture is pale yellow and thickened about map[index:1 type:timer].

  4. Mix the Mascarpone Cheese into the egg yolk mixture until smooth and creamy about map[index:2 type:timer].

  5. Fold the egg whites into the mascarpone mixture until well combined, being careful not to deflate the egg whites.

  6. Rinse out the flat container and mix in the Espresso Powder, room temp Water, and Marsala Wine

  7. Dunk the Ladyfinger Cookies into the espresso mixture for map[index:3 type:timer] and place them in a rectangulardish. Spread half of the mascarpone mixture over the ladyfingers and smooth it out with a spatula. Repeat the process with the remaining ladyfingers and mascarpone mixture.

  8. Refrigerate the tiramisu for at least map[index:4 type:timer] or overnight to allow the flavors to meld together.

  9. Dust the top of the tiramisu with Cocoa Powder before serving.
