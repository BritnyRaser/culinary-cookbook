Beef Tenderloin with Red Wine Sauce

Prospects, American, Beef, Roast, Tenderloin, Red Wine

Ingredients

  1. Beef Tenderloin Roast — 1 (2.5 lb)
  2. Unsalted Butter — 5 tbsp
  3. Shallots — 3/4 cup
  4. Red Wine — 1 1/4 cups
  5. Beef Broth — 3 cups
  6. Fresh Thyme Sprigs — 6
  7. Salt — 1/4 tsp
  8. Black Pepper — 1/8 tsp
  9. Sugar — 1 tsp
  10. Unsalted Butter — 3 tbsp
  11. Flour — 2 tbsp
  12. Salt — 1 1/2 tsp
  13. Black Pepper — 3/4 tsp
  14. Vegetable Oil — 2 tbsp
  15. Beef Broth — 1/4 cup

Cookware

  1. medium saucepan
  2. small bowl
  3. spoon
  4. oven-proof skillet
  5. tongs
  6. carving board
  7. wooden spoon

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 400°F with an oven rack set in the middle position.

  2. Let Beef Tenderloin Roast stand at room temperature for map[index:0 type:timer] before roasting.

  3. Melt Unsalted Butter in a medium saucepan. Add finely chopped Shallots and cook over medium-low heat until soft and translucent, map[index:1 type:timer].

  4. Add Red Wine, Beef Broth, Fresh Thyme Sprigs, Salt, Black Pepper, and Sugar. Bring to a boil, then cook over medium heat for map[index:2 type:timer], or until the liquid is reduced by half.

  5. In a small bowl, combine remaining Unsalted Butter (softened) and Flour. Mix together with a spoon into a smooth paste (beurre manié).

  6. Reduce heat to low and remove the thyme sprigs from the reduced wine sauce. Whisk the flour-butter paste into the simmering liquid a teaspoonful at a time, and simmer for map[index:3 type:timer] until the sauce is thickened. Set aside.

  7. Season the beef tenderloin all over with Salt (1/2 tsp per lb) and Black Pepper (1/4 tsp per lb).

  8. Heat Vegetable Oil in an oven-proof skillet over medium-high heat until almost smoking. Cook the beef, turning with tongs, until well browned on all but one side, map[index:4 type:timer] total.

  9. Turn the tenderloin so that the un-seared side is down, and transfer the skillet directly to the preheated oven.

  10. Roast until a thermometer inserted into the center of the meat registers 120°F-125°F for medium-rare, map[index:5 type:timer].

  11. Transfer the beef to a carving board and let it rest, covered loosely with aluminum foil, for map[index:6 type:timer].

  12. Carefully discard the excess fat from the skillet. Place the skillet on the stovetop over medium heat, add Beef Broth, and bring to a boil while scraping up the browned bits from the bottom of the pan with a wooden spoon.

  13. Pour the deglazed pan juices into the wine sauce and bring to a simmer.

  14. Carve the beef tenderloin into 1/3-inch-thick slices and serve with the warm red wine sauce.
