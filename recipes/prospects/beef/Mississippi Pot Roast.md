---
Title: Mississippi Pot Roast
Servings: 8 servings
Tags: Slow Cooker, American
Link: https://www.spendwithpennies.com/mississippi-pot-roast/
---

In a #large skillet{}, over medium-high heat, brown the @Beef Chuck Roast or Pot Roast{4-5 lb} for ~{5 minutes} per side.

Place roast in the bottom of a 6-quart #slow cooker{} & pour in the @Pepperoncini Juice{1/2 cup}.

Sprinkle the dry @Ranch Seasoning Mix{1 oz} & @Au Jus Gravy Mix or Brown Gravy Mix{1 oz} over the top of the roast.

Add the @Pepperoncini Peppers{6} & sliced @Butter{1/4 cup} on top of the roast.

Cover & cook on low for ~{8-10 hours} or until the roast is fork-tender.

Once the roast is cooked, shred it with a #fork{} & mix it into the juices in the slow cooker.

Serve over mashed potatoes, egg noodles, or rice.
