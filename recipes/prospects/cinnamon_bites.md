---
Title: Cinnamon Bites
Tags: Bakery, Protein
---

Mix in a #bowl:
- @Flour{107g}
- @Baking Powder{1 tsp}
- @Cinnamon Powder{1 tsp}
- @Salt

Add @Greek Yogurt{1 cup} & mix well until a dough forms.

Divide dough into small balls (about 20)& air fry at 350°F for 15 minutes or until golden brown.

In a #small bowl{}, mix together @Cinnamon Powder{1 Tablespoon} & @Sugar{1/3 cup}.

Lightly spray with @Oil & coat with the Cinnamon mixture
