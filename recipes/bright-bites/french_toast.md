---
title: French Toast
tags: American
---

Crack @Eggs{3-4} onto a #plate or #bowl

Add @Milk & stir into the eggs

Take @Bread Slices{4} & place into the egg mixture to coat both sides of the bread

Heat a #pan with @Butter coated on it

Place the bread slices into the pan

Flip when needed until both sides are golden brown

Serve with @Maple Syrup{} (@Butter & @Powdered Sugar{} are optional)
