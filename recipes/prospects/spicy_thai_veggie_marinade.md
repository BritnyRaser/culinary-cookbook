---
Title: Spicy Thai Veggie Marinade
Servings: 1 servings
Tags: Prospects, Thai, Sauce
Link: https://www.themanual.com/food-and-drink/marinades-for-beef-pork-chicken-seafood-veggies/
---

Place the @Cilantro{1 1/2 cup}, @Garlic{8 cloves}, @Fish Sauce{2 Tablespoons}, @Water{1 Tablespoon}, @Black Pepper{1 teaspoon}, @Chili-Garlic Sauce{1 Tablespoon} in a #food processor{}; pulse to form a paste.

With the processor running, pour the @Oil{1/3 cup} through the lid spout in a slow stream.

Use a #spatula to scrape the marinade out of the processor and pour it over the vegetables.

Toss well, cover, and refrigerate for ~{1 hour}.
