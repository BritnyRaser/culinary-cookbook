Jello Beans

Jello Beans

Munchies, Homemade, Jello

Ingredients

  1. Water — 1 1/4 cup
  2. Small Boxes of Jello — 2

Instructions

  1. Boiling Water and Small Boxes of Jello stirred until dissolved.

  2. Put in microwave for 3 minutes until it's boiling.
