Grilled PB & J

Ingredients

Directions

  1. Put Jam on 1 side of a Bread Slice

  2. Put Peanut Butter on the other bread

  3. Place the breads together so spread is on the inside

  4. Butter exterior sides of the breads

  5. Heat a pan to medium heat

  6. Place the sandwich onto the hot pan and flip when necessary to reach golden sides
