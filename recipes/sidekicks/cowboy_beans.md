--- 
Title: Cowboy Beans
Servings: 8
Tags: Beef, Pork
Link: https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/13795/calico-beans/
---

Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F

Cook @Ground Beef{1 lb} & chopped @Bacon{1 cup} in a #large skillet{} over medium-high heat until evenly brown. Drain & transfer meat to a 4-quart #casserole dish or slow cooker{}. 

Add @Pork & Beans{15 oz}, @Kidney Beans{15 oz}, @Butter Beans{15 oz}, @Lima Beans{15 oz}, @Brown Sugar{1 cup}, chopped @Onion{1}, @Ketchup{1/2 cup}, @Vinegar{3 Tablespoons}, & @Mustard{1 Tablespoon} to the dish; mix well. 

Bake, covered, in the preheated oven until bubbly & heated through, about ~{1 hour}. 

Review Recommendations: add Worcestershire Sauce; BBQ sauce instead of Ketchup; add Chili Beans; less Sugar can substitute with Molasses
