Mojo Chicken

Ingredients

Directions

  1. Add all of your ingredients for the Mojo Marinade (except Serrano Peppers & Olive Oil) to a blender: Navel Oranges, Lime Juice and Zest, Grapefruit, Cilantro, Garlic Cloves, Fresh Oregano, Garlic Herb Seasoning, Ground Cumin, Kosher Salt, and Black Pepper.

  2. Blend for map[index:0 type:timer] in order to begin the mixture, then add the Olive Oil.

  3. Blend until fully immersed, then once done add the sliced Serrano Peppers.

  4. Add 1/4 cup of the marinade into a separate small bowl to reserve for later before adding to chicken.

  5. Add your Chicken Quarters to a food safe bowl and pour the remaining marinade over the top and mix thoroughly.

  6. Cover and place in the fridge, along with reserve marinade, to sit for map[index:1 type:timer] but ideally overnight.

  7. Preheat your grill for two-zone indirect cooking at 300 degrees F.

  8. Add your chicken to the high heat side of the grill to sear for map[index:2 type:timer] per side.

  9. Once the chicken has been seared, pull them to the cool side of the grill to finish cooking.

  10. Cook for map[index:3 type:timer] until they reach 170 degrees F internal. About map[index:4 type:timer] before the chicken is done, baste them with the reserved marinade.

  11. Pull the chicken off once done to rest for map[index:5 type:timer].

  12. Serve the chicken with White Rice, Black Beans, sliced limes, and Chopped Cilantro.
