Egg Smoothie

Ingredients

Directions

  1. Add Soft-Boiled Eggs, Half And Half, Sweetener, Vanilla Extract, and Salt to a blender and blend until smooth.

  2. Taste and adjust optional sweetener as needed.

  3. Note: If your eggs are medium or hard-boiled, add another splash or two of liquid and thin the smoothie to your liking. Use splash Apple Cider Vinegar while boiling the eggs to help with the sulfur smell.
