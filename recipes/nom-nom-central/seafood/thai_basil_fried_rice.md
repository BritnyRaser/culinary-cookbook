---
Title: Thai Basil Fried Rice
Servings: 2-3 people
Tags: Thai
Link: https://seonkyounglongest.com/thai-basil-fried-rice/
---

Pound @Garlic{4 cloves} & @Thai Chilies{3} together using a #mortar & pestle{}. (If you don't have a mortar & pestle, carefully smash them with a knife & chop.)

Slice @Shallot{1} & set aside along with the garlic & chili mixture.

In a #small mixing bowl{}, mix @Oyster Sauce{1 tbsp}, @Fish Sauce{1 tbsp}, @Palm Sugar{1 tsp}, & @Black or White Pepper{1 pinch}. Set the sauce aside.

Heat a #wok{} (or #large skillet{}) over high heat until smoking. Add @Butter{1 tbsp} & let it melt completely until browned & smoky.

Add the garlic & chili mixture, sliced shallot, & @Shrimp{4 oz}. Stir-fry & cook the shrimp for ~{1 minute} on each side, then mix everything together in the wok & push to one side.

Crack @Eggs{2} into the wok. Let the eggs cook slightly, then scramble. When the eggs are cooked but still silky, toss everything in the wok together.

Add cold @Day-Old Jasmine Rice{2 cups} & pour the prepared sauce mixture over top. Toss everything together until the rice is evenly coated with the sauce, about ~{2-3 minutes}.

Toss in @Thai Basil Leaves{1 cup} & turn off the heat. Keep tossing until the basil is wilted & well combined with the fried rice.

Transfer to a #serving dish{} & enjoy hot.
