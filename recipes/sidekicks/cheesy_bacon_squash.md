--- 
Title: Cheesy Bacon Squash
Tags: Sidekicks
---

Mix in #Dutch Oven{}:
- @Butternut Squash{2 lb}
- minced @Garlic{3 cloves}
- @Olive Oil{1 Tablespoon}
- @Rosemary{1 teaspoon}
- @Salt
- @Black Pepper{}

Top the mixture with @Bacon{4 slices} & @Butter{1/2 stick}.

Bake at 425F for 45 minutes.

Sprinkle with @Parmesaon Cheese{1/2 cup} and @Cheddar Cheese{1/2 cup}.

Bake for an additional 10 minutes until cheese is melted and bubbly.

Top with @Parsley{} & serve.
