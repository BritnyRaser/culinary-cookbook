Thai Basil Fried Rice

Thai Basil Fried Rice

Nom Nom Central, Thai, Rice, Seafood, Shrimp

Ingredients

  1. Garlic — 4 cloves
  2. Thai Chilies — 3
  3. Shallot — 1
  4. Oyster Sauce — 1 tbsp
  5. Fish Sauce — 1 tbsp
  6. Palm Sugar — 1 tsp
  7. Black or White Pepper — 1 pinch
  8. Butter — 1 tbsp
  9. Shrimp — 4 oz
  10. Eggs — 2
  11. Day-Old Jasmine Rice — 2 cups
  12. Thai Basil Leaves — 1 cup

Cookware

  1. mortar and pestle
  2. small mixing bowl
  3. wok
  4. large skillet
  5. serving dish

Instructions

  1. Pound Garlic and Thai Chilies together using a mortar and pestle. (If you don't have a mortar and pestle, carefully smash them with a knife and chop.)

  2. Slice Shallot and set aside along with the garlic and chili mixture.

  3. In a small mixing bowl, mix Oyster Sauce, Fish Sauce, Palm Sugar, and Black or White Pepper. Set the sauce aside.

  4. Heat a wok (or large skillet) over high heat until smoking. Add Butter and let it melt completely until browned and smoky.

  5. Add the garlic and chili mixture, sliced shallot, and Shrimp. Stir-fry and cook the shrimp for map[index:0 type:timer] on each side, then mix everything together in the wok and push to one side.

  6. Crack Eggs into the wok. Let the eggs cook slightly, then scramble. When the eggs are cooked but still silky, toss everything in the wok together.

  7. Add cold Day-Old Jasmine Rice and pour the prepared sauce mixture over top. Toss everything together until the rice is evenly coated with the sauce, about map[index:1 type:timer].

  8. Toss in Thai Basil Leaves and turn off the heat. Keep tossing until the basil is wilted and well combined with the fried rice.

  9. Transfer to a serving dish and enjoy hot.
