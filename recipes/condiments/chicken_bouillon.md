---
Title: Chicken Bouillon
Tags: Condiments, Seasoning
---

Mix in a #large bowl{}:
- @Nutritional Yeast Flakes{1/3 cup}
- @Salt{1/4 cup}
- @Garlic Powder{1/4 cup}
- @Onion Powder{1/4 cup}
- @Basil{1/4 cup}
- @Oregano{1/4 cup}
- @Rosemary{1 1/2 Tablespoon}
- @Black Pepper{}

Optional: Can blend in a food processor for a finer texture. 

Store in an airtight container. Use as a seasoning for soups, stews, and other dishes.
