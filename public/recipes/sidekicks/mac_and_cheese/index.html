Mac & Cheese

Ingredients

Directions

  1. Noodles: Boil Noodles in pot

  2. Cheese: In separate pan, melt Butter.

  3. Add Flour and whick until mixed.

  4. Slowly add Milk and wisk fpr 4-6 minutes

  5. Add shredded Cheese slowly while whisking on no heat!

  6. Add Garlic Powder. If seafood, also add Old Bay and Creole

  7. Smoke 225 degrees for map[index:0 type:timer] with Applewood or Pecan wood chunks
