---
Title: Teriyaki Sauce
Servings: 1 cup
Tags: Japanese, Sauce
Link: https://www.justonecookbook.com/teriyaki-sauce/
---

In a #saucepan{}, add @Sake{1/2 cup} & @Mirin{1/2 cup}.

Add @Soy Sauce{1/2 cup} & @Sugar{1/4 cup}.

Bring the mixture to a boil over medium heat & continuously stir the sauce until the sugar is dissolved.

Once boiling, lower the heat to medium-low & simmer for ~{10-15 minutes} or until the sauce is thickened & small bubbles start to rise & appear on the surface.

Pour the sauce into a #sterilized jar{} & leave uncovered to cool.
