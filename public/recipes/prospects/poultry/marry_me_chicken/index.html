Marry Me Chicken

Marry Me Chicken

Prospects, Chicken, Italian-American,

Ingredients

  1. Paprika — 1
  2. Parsley — 1 tsp
  3. Salt — 1/2 tsp
  4. Pepper — 1/2 tsp
  5. Chicken Thighs or Breasts — 5-6 pieces
  6. Flour — 1/4 cup
  7. Butter — 2 Tablespoons
  8. Shallot — 1
  9. Garlic — 4 cloves
  10. Red Chili Flakes — 1 teaspoon
  11. Thyme — 1 teaspoon
  12. Oregano — 1 teaspoon
  13. Sun Dried Tomatoes — 1/3 cup
  14. Heavy Cream — 1 cup
  15. Chicken Stock — 1/2 cup
  16. Parmesan Cheese — 3/4 cup
  17. Basil Leaves — 1/4 cup

Cookware

  1. small bowl
  2. large skillet
  3. plate

Instructions

  1. Mix all the chicken seasoning ingredients in a small bowl: Paprika, Parsley Salt Pepper

  2. Season the Chicken Thighs or Breasts on both sides.

  3. Dredge the chicken in Flour and shake off any excess.

  4. In a large skillet, melt the Butter on medium heat.

  5. Brown the chicken, skin side first for map[index:0 type:timer] on each side, or until golden brown. Transfer onto a plate and set aside.

  6. In the same pan, add a little more butter if needed and sauté chopped Shallot for a minute.

  7. Add Garlic and cook for map[index:1 type:timer], or until it's fragrant.

  8. Add Red Chili Flakes, Thyme and Oregano and mix well.

  9. Add in chopped Sun Dried Tomatoes and cook for map[index:2 type:timer].

  10. Adjust the heat to medium-low, and add the Heavy Cream, Chicken Stock and the Parmesan Cheese to the skillet. Mix and stir well till the cheese melts, and simmer it for map[index:3 type:timer].

  11. Mix in Basil Leaves and place the chicken thighs in skin side up. Adjust the heat to medium low, cover, and cook till chicken is cooked through, stirring the sauce occasionally. Internal temperature for chicken thighs reaches 80-190°F and chicken breast cook till internal temperature reaches 165°F.

  12. Garnish with some more basil leaves and serve immediately.
