Chex Mix

Munchies, Homemade, Chex Cereal, Pretzels, Peanuts, Butter, Worcestershire, Lawry's Salt, Garlic Powder

Ingredients

  1. Wheat, Rice, Corn Chex Cereal — 10 cups
  2. Pretzels — 2 cups
  3. Peanuts — 2 cups
  4. Butter — 6 Tablespoons
  5. Worcestershire — 5 Tablespoons
  6. Lawry's Salt — 1 1/2 teaspoons
  7. Garlic Powder — 3/4 cup

Cookware

  1. turkey oven bag
  2. baking sheet

Instructions

  1. Oven set at 250

  2. Mix together Wheat, Rice, Corn Chex Cereal, Pretzels, Peanuts, Butter, Worcestershire, Lawry's Salt, Garlic Powder in turkey oven bag

  3. Place mixture onto baking sheet and bake for map[index:0 type:timer], stir every 15 minutes.
