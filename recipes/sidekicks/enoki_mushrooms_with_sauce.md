--- 
Title: Enoki Mushrooms with Sauce
---

Be gentle when handling these @Enoki Mushrooms{14 oz}.  Trim away about 1 inch of the root section. Use your fingers to tear the Enoki Mushrooms into small bute size bundles & line them up neatly.  Rinse clean & drain.

Prepare a wok with boiling @Water, & blanch the Enoki Mushrooms in 2 batches, cooking each batch for about 1 minute.  Drain off the Water & tansfer the Mushrooms to your serving plate.

In a #small saucepan{}, heat the @Seasame Oil{2 Tablespoon} over mediumm heat.  Add the @Garlic{3 Cloves}, & cook for about 10 seconds (no need to brown the Garlic).  

Add the @Light Soy Sauce{3 Tablespoon}, @Sugar{1/2 teaspoon}, @Ginger{1/2 Tablespoon}, & @Green Onion{} - we want that fresh & sweet taste!

Slowly pour the sauce over the Enoki Mushrooms & serve immediately.  Enjoy!
