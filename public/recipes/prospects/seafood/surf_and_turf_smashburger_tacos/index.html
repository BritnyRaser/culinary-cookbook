Surf and Turf Smashburger Tacos

Prospects, American, Beef, Ground Beef, Shrimp, Tacos, Grilled

Ingredients

  1. Mayonnaise — 3/4 cup
  2. Chipotles in Adobo — 1 tbsp
  3. Lime Juice — 1
  4. Salt
  5. Ground Beef 80/20 — 1 lb
  6. Small Flour Tortillas — 8
  7. Smoked Chipotle Mezcal Rub — 2 tbsp
  8. Shrimp — 1 lb
  9. Neutral Oil — 1 tbsp
  10. Smoked Chipotle Mezcal Rub
  11. Cabbage — 1 cup
  12. Pickled Jalapenos

Cookware

  1. grill
  2. small bowl
  3. bowl
  4. skewers

Instructions

  1. Fire up the grill. Light the coals and set up the grill for 2-zone cooking, placing the hot coals on one side only. Spread the coals evenly when ready and allow the grill to preheat.

  2. Mix the ingredients for the Chipotle Mayo in a small bowl: Mayonnaise, chopped Chipotles in Adobo, Lime Juice, and Salt. Taste and adjust as needed.

  3. Portion the Ground Beef 80/20 into golf-ball sized portions (roughly 2 ounces each). Using wax paper, press the meat directly into the Small Flour Tortillas, pushing the beef out to the edges of the tortillas. Season the meat as needed with Smoked Chipotle Mezcal Rub (split between beef and shrimp).

  4. Prepare the shrimp skewers. Place the cleaned and shelled Shrimp in a bowl. Pour in Neutral Oil and add enough of the remaining Smoked Chipotle Mezcal Rub to coat the shrimp. Mix them around to spread the seasoning, and thread them onto skewers. If using wood skewers, make sure they soak in water prior to grilling.

  5. Place the tacos on the grill over the coals, 2 at a time. Cook for map[index:0 type:timer], watching closely, then flip them to the cool side of the grill and allow them to rest. The ambient heat will continue to cook the backside without charring the tortillas. Repeat the process for the rest of the tacos.

  6. Clean the grill grates and place the shrimp skewers on the grill. Cook for map[index:1 type:timer] per side, or until they are charred and reach 120°F internal temperature.

  7. Build the tacos by placing the cooked shrimp on top of the smashburger tacos and filling with chopped Cabbage, Pickled Jalapenos, and the prepared Chipotle Mayo.
