---
Title: Egg Smoothie
Servings: 2 servings
Tags: American, Carnivore, Keto
Link: https://www.primaledgehealth.com/egg-smoothie/
---

Add @Soft-Boiled Eggs{5}, @Half & Half{4 oz}, @Sweetener{2 1/2 tablespoon}, @Vanilla Extract{1 tsp}, & @Salt{1/8 tsp} to a #blender & blend until smooth.

Taste & adjust optional sweetener as needed. 

Note: If your eggs are medium or hard-boiled, add another splash or two of liquid & thin the smoothie to your liking. Use splash Apple Cider Vinegar while boiling the eggs to help with the sulfur smell.
