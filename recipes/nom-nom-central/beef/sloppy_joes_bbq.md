Tags: American, Homemade

Brown @Ground Beef{1 lb} in a #skillet until fully cooked.

Stir in @Hunt's Ketchup{16 oz}, @Ground Cloves{}, @Ground Allspice{}, @Ground Cinnamon{}, & @Black Pepper{}. 

Cook until the sauce is well combined & the mixture is warm & flavorful.
