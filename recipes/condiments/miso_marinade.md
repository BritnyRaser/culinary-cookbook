---
Title: Miso Marinade
Servings: 1 servings
Tags: Japanese, Sauce
Link: https://www.themanual.com/food-&-drink/marinades-for-beef-pork-chicken-seafood-veggies/
---

Bring the @Sake{1/4 cup} & @Mirin{1/4 cup} to a boil in a #medium saucepan{} over high heat.

Boil for ~{20 seconds} to evaporate the alcohol.

Turn the heat down to low, add the @Miso Paste{1/4 cup}, & #whisk.

When the miso has dissolved completely, turn the heat up to high again & add the @Sugar{3 Tablespoons}, whisking constantly to ensure that the sugar does not burn on the bottom of the pan.

Remove from heat once the sugar is fully dissolved.

Cool to room temperature.
