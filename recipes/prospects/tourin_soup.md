---
Title: Tourin Soup
Tags: Prospects, Sidekicks

---

Cut the top off half a head of @Garlic{1}.

Drizzle with a little @Duck Fat{} or olive oil.

Wrap in foil and roast at 400°F for ~{40 minutes}.

Squeeze out the cloves and mash with a fork.

Heat @Duck Fat{3 Tablespoons} in a #saucepan over medium-low heat.

Add @Onion{1}, raw @Garlic{1}, @Bay Leaf{1}, and @Thyme{0.5 teaspoons}.

Cook gently for ~{10 minutes} until softened without browning.

Add @Flour{1.5 Tablespoons} and stir for ~{1 minute}.

Gradually whisk in @Chicken Stock{2.5 cups}.

Add mashed roasted garlic.

Simmer gently for ~{20 minutes} until garlic is very tender.

Whisk @Egg Yolks{2} with @Sherry Vinegar{3/4 Tablespoon}.

Slowly whisk the Egg Yolk mixture in a ladleful of hot soup to temper.

Remove soup from heat & slowly stir the yolk mixture into the soup.

Lightly whisk @Egg Whites{2} until loosened.

Slowly drizzle into the hot soup while gently stirring.

Let the residual heat cook the whites into delicate ribbons. If needed, use very low heat until fully cooked, without boiling.

Serve with with grated @Gruyère, fresh thyme, and @Black pepper{}.
