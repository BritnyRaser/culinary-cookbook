---
Title: Garlic Cloud Bread
Tags: Prospects, Bakery, Protein
---

Whisk @Egg Whites{1 cup} until they whisk peaks/thickened like whipped cream.

Gently fold your @Cheese{1 cup}, @Garlic Powder{1/2 Tablespoon}, @Herb Seasoning{1/2 Tablespoon}, @Onion Powder{1 Tablespoon}, and @Salt{1/2 teaspoon} into the egg whites.

Place dollops onto a parchment paper in #air fryer{} and air fry at 375°F for 15 minutes or until golden brown.

Optional: Brush with @Garlic Butter{}
