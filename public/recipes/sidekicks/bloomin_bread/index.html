Bloomin' Bread

Sidekicks, Bread, Prosciutto, Parsley, Cheese, Onion, Oil

Ingredients

  1. Bread — 1
  2. Oil — 3 Tablespoons
  3. Garlic — 5 Tablespoons
  4. Onion — 5 Tablespoons
  5. Prosciutto
  6. Cheese — 2 cups
  7. Parsley

Cookware

  1. small bowl

Instructions

  1. Set oven to 370F

  2. Cut Bread surface imto square design

  3. Whisk Oil, minced Garlic & Onion in a small bowl

  4. Slice Prosciutto

  5. Apply the Oil Paste to top of crevasses of th Bread while stuffing in Prosciutto and Cheese

  6. Bake for map[index:0 type:timer]

  7. Garnish with Parsley
