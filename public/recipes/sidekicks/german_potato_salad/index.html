German Potato Salad

Sidekicks, Homemade, Red Potatoes, Sugar, Oil, Vinegar, Onion

Ingredients

  1. Red Potatoes — 5 lbs
  2. Salt
  3. Pepper

Instructions

  1. Boil Red Potatoes until soft & peel while warm

  2. Dice the potatoes

  3. Sprinkle Salt & Pepper across

  4. Mix gently & not often. Cover up & let melt together for map[index:0 type:timer]

  5. Taste & adjust as needed
