Cheez-Its

Ingredients

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 250°F.

  2. Cut Cheddar Cheese into quarters and spread out on a parchment-lined baking sheet (about an inch apart) (Optional) To resemble Cheez-its, you can add a hole to the center of each using a straw

  3. Sprinkle with salt.

  4. Put into oven and bake for roughly map[index:0 type:timer]. You want to to look for a golden brown color.

  5. Remove from oven and let cool for 10-15 minutes.
