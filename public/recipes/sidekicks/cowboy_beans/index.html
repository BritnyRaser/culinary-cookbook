Cowboy Beans

Ingredients

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F

  2. Cook Ground Beef and chopped Bacon in a large skillet over medium-high heat until evenly brown. Drain and transfer meat to a 4-quart casserole dish or slow cooker.

  3. Add Pork and Beans, Kidney Beans, Butter Beans, Lima Beans, Brown Sugar, chopped Onion, Ketchup, Vinegar, and Mustard to the dish; mix well.

  4. Bake, covered, in the preheated oven until bubbly and heated through, about map[index:0 type:timer].

  5. Review Recommendations: add Worcestershire Sauce; BBQ sauce instead of Ketchup; add Chili Beans; less Sugar can substitute with Molasses
