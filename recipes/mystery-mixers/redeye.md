---
Title: Redeye
Tags: Homemade, German
---

In a #large pot{}, melt @White Sugar{1.5 cups} over low heat until it boils & turns a deep brown color; the darker it gets, the darker the Redeye will be.

Add @Water{2 quarts} & bring it back to a boil.

Let it cool, then stir in @Everclear{1 quart}.

Funnel the mixture into #bottles{3}.
