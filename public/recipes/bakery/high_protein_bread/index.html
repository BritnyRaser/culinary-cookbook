High Protein Bread

High Protein Bread

Bakery, Bread, Greek Yogurt, Protein

Ingredients

  1. flour — 3 cups
  2. baking powder — 1 Tablespoon
  3. salt — 1 1/4 teaspoons
  4. baking soda — 1/2 teaspoon
  5. milk — 1.5 cups
  6. Greek yogurt — 3/4 cup

Cookware

  1. loaf pan
  2. parchment paper
  3. large bowl
  4. bowl
  5. rubber spatula
  6. wire rack

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F Line a 9x5 inch loaf pan with parchment paper. Set aside.

  2. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, salt and baking soda.

  3. In a separate bowl, mix together the milk and Greek yogurt, then pour into the flour mixture.

  4. Combine the wet and dry ingredients with a rubber spatula until combined, then spread the thick dough evenly into the prepared loaf pan.

  5. Brush the top with a little milk and bake for map[index:0 type:timer], or until a tester inserted in the center comes out clean. If the top starts to get too dark, cover with a piece of foil for the remainder of the baking time.

  6. Let cool for map[index:1 type:timer] before transferring to a wire rack to cool completely.

  7. Enjoy the bread as is or toasted with butter and jam. Store covered for up to 3 days at room temperature. It also freezes really well for up to 8 weeks.
