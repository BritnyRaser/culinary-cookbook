Aged Eggnog

Aged Eggnog

Prospects, American, Cocktail, Eggs, Rum, Bourbon, Cognac

Ingredients

  1. Eggs Yolk — 12 large
  2. Sugar — 1 lb
  3. Freshly Grated Nutmeg — 1 tsp
  4. Half-and-Half — 1 pint
  5. Whole Milk — 1 pint
  6. Heavy Cream — 1 pint
  7. Jamaican Rum — 1 cup
  8. Cognac — 1 cup
  9. Bourbon — 1 cup
  10. Kosher Salt — 1/4 tsp
  11. Freshly Grated Nutmeg — for garnish

Cookware

  1. large mixing bowl
  2. bowl
  3. pitcher
  4. large glass jar

Instructions

  1. Separate the Eggs Yolk and store the whites for another application.

  2. Beat the egg yolks with Sugar and Freshly Grated Nutmeg in a large mixing bowl until the mixture lightens in color and falls off the whisk in a solid ribbon.

  3. Combine Half-and-Half, Whole Milk, Heavy Cream, Jamaican Rum, Cognac, Bourbon, and Kosher Salt in a second bowl or pitcher.

  4. Slowly beat the dairy and alcohol mixture into the egg yolk mixture.

  5. Transfer the mixture to a large glass jar (or a couple of smaller ones) and store in the fridge for a minimum of 2 weeks. A month or two is even better.

  6. Serve in mugs or cups topped with extra Freshly Grated Nutmeg.
