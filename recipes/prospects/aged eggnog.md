---
Title: Aged Eggnog
Servings: 8-10 servings
Tags: American, Cocktail
Link: https://altonbrown.com/recipes/aged-eggnog/
---

Separate the @Eggs Yolk{12 large} & store the whites for another application.

Beat the egg yolks with @Sugar{1 lb} & @Freshly Grated Nutmeg{1 tsp} in a #large mixing bowl{} until the mixture lightens in color & falls off the whisk in a solid ribbon.

Combine @Half-&-Half{1 pint}, @Whole Milk{1 pint}, @Heavy Cream{1 pint}, @Jamaican Rum{1 cup}, @Cognac{1 cup}, @Bourbon{1 cup}, & @Kosher Salt{1/4 tsp} in a second #bowl{} or #pitcher{}.

Slowly beat the dairy & alcohol mixture into the egg yolk mixture.

Transfer the mixture to a #large glass jar{} (or a couple of smaller ones) & store in the fridge for a minimum of 2 weeks. A month or two is even better.

Serve in mugs or cups topped with extra @Freshly Grated Nutmeg{for garnish}.
