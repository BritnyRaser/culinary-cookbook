Neopolitan Pizza Dough

Neopolitan Pizza Dough

Nom Nom Central, Italian, Tipo 00 Flour, Water, Salt, Instant Dry Yeast

Ingredients

  1. Flour — 620g
  2. Water — 380g
  3. Salt — 14g
  4. Dry Yeast — 0.5g

Cookware

  1. large bowl
  2. wooden spoon
  3. damp cloth
  4. kitchen scales
  5. dough scraper
  6. small bowl

Instructions

  1. Mix Flour, Water, Salt, Dry Yeast into a shaggy mass in a large bowl, starting by adding the water first. You can do this by hand or use a wooden spoon.

  2. Cover the bowl with a damp cloth (if the cloth is not damp the dough may dry out).

  3. Leave the dough to rest for around map[index:0 type:timer] (the technical term for this process is the Autolyse).

  4. Turn the dough out onto the counter and knead for around map[index:1 type:timer].

  5. Place the dough back into the bowl (or proofing box) and cover tightly.

  6. Leave the dough to proof for around 20 hours at room temperature.

  7. Divide the dough into 4 equal parts (250g each) using some kitchen scales and a dough scraper. Do this about 4 hours before you intend to cook the pizza.

  8. Place each dough ball into a small bowl (or a proofing box) and cover. Alternatively, use a large tupperware container with a lid, or two smaller ones.

  9. Leave the dough balls to proof again for about 4-6 hours.

  10. Note: use high protein 12-14% Flour, if use 00 flour then 640g with 360g Water
