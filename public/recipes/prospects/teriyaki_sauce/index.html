Teriyaki Sauce

Ingredients

Directions

  1. In a saucepan, add Sake and Mirin.

  2. Add Soy Sauce and Sugar.

  3. Bring the mixture to a boil over medium heat and continuously stir the sauce until the sugar is dissolved.

  4. Once boiling, lower the heat to medium-low and simmer for map[index:0 type:timer] or until the sauce is thickened and small bubbles start to rise and appear on the surface.

  5. Pour the sauce into a sterilized jar and leave uncovered to cool.
