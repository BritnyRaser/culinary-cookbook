Bourbon Pork Marinade

Prospects, American, Sauce

Ingredients

  1. Bourbon — 1 cup
  2. Brown Sugar — 1/2 cup
  3. Soy Sauce — 1/4 cup
  4. Worcestershire Sauce — 1 Tablespoon
  5. Rosemary — 1/2 Tablespoon
  6. Black Pepper — 1 teaspoon

Instructions

  1. Whisk or blend Bourbon, Brown Sugar, Soy Sauce, Worcestershire Sauce, chopped Rosemary, Black Pepper together until fully incorporated.

  2. Marinate pork for up to four hours.
