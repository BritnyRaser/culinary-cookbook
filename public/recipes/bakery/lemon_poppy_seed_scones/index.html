Lemon Poppy Seed Scones

Lemon Poppy Seed Scones

Bakery, American, Lemon, Flour, Sugar, Butter, Heavy Cream, Egg, Baking Powder, Salt, Lemon Extract, Poppy Seeds, Powdered Sugar, Vegetarian, Breakfast, Dessert

Ingredients

  1. Flour — 2 cups
  2. Sugar — 1/2 cup
  3. Baking Powder — 3 teaspoons
  4. Baking Soda — 1/2 teaspoon
  5. Salt — 1/2 teaspoon
  6. Poppy Seeds — 1 1/2 Tablespoons
  7. Butter — 6 Tablespoons
  8. Heavy Whipping Cream — 2/3 cup
  9. Egg — 1
  10. Lemon Juice — 2 Tablespoons
  11. Lemon Extract — 3 teaspoons
  12. Lemon Peel — 1 teaspoon
  13. Water — 1/2 Tablespoon
  14. Lemon Juice — 1 Tablespoon
  15. Lemon Peel — 1 teaspoon
  16. Powdered Sugar — 1 cup

Cookware

  1. medium bowl
  2. toothpick
  3. cooling rack
  4. small bowl

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees F.

  2. Combine Flour, Sugar, Baking Powder, Baking Soda, Salt, and Poppy Seeds in a medium bowl.

  3. Cut Butter into chunks and use a pastry cutter to combine with flour mixture until butter chunks are pea-sized.

  4. Add Heavy Whipping Cream, Egg, Lemon Juice, Lemon Extract and Lemon Peel. Mix until just combined.

  5. Turn onto a hard, floured surface and knead until dough forms, about map[index:0 type:timer]. Dough will be a little sticky. Form into a ball and pat down into a circle about 1 inch thick.

  6. Cut circle into eight triangles and place on a lightly greased baking sheet.

  7. Bake for map[index:1 type:timer], or until bottoms of scones are lightly browned and a toothpick comes out clean. Transfer to cooling rack.

  8. Let scones cool for map[index:2 type:timer]. While they are cooling, prepare the glaze. Combine Water, Lemon Juice, and Lemon Peel in small bowl. Add Powdered Sugar 1 tablespoon at a time, mix thoroughly before adding more.

  9. Once cool, spoon glaze onto scones.
