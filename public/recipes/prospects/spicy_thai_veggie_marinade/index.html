Spicy Thai Veggie Marinade

Ingredients

Directions

  1. Place the Cilantro, Garlic, Fish Sauce, Water, Black Pepper, Chili-Garlic Sauce in a food processor; pulse to form a paste.

  2. With the processor running, pour the Oil through the lid spout in a slow stream.

  3. Use a spatula to scrape the marinade out of the processor and pour it over the vegetables.

  4. Toss well, cover, and refrigerate for map[index:0 type:timer].
