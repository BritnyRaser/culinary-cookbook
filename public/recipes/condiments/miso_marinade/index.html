Miso Marinade

Condiments, Japanese, Sauce

Ingredients

  1. Sake — 1/4 cup
  2. Mirin — 1/4 cup
  3. Miso Paste — 1/4 cup
  4. Sugar — 3 Tablespoons

Cookware

  1. medium saucepan
  2. whisk

Instructions

  1. Bring the Sake and Mirin to a boil in a medium saucepan over high heat.

  2. Boil for map[index:0 type:timer] to evaporate the alcohol.

  3. Turn the heat down to low, add the Miso Paste, and whisk.

  4. When the miso has dissolved completely, turn the heat up to high again and add the Sugar, whisking constantly to ensure that the sugar does not burn on the bottom of the pan.

  5. Remove from heat once the sugar is fully dissolved.

  6. Cool to room temperature.
