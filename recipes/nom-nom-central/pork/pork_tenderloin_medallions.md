---
Title: Pork Tenderloin Medallions
Tags: American
---

Slice the @Pork Tenderloin{2-4 lbs} into medallions. Pound each piece with a #mallet{} until it is about 1/8 inch thick.

Dredge the pork in @Flour{1/2 cup}, then season with @Salt & @Black Pepper{}.

Bring water to a boil in a #large pot{} & prepare the mashed @Potatoes{}.

Heat @Oil{} in a #skillet{} over medium-high heat.

Cook the pork until golden brown, about ~{3 minutes} per side. Transfer it to a plate & set aside.

Add @Butter{4 tablespoons}, @Flour{4 tablespoons}, & @Mushrooms{2 lbs} to the skillet. Saute for about ~{1 minute}.

Pour in @Marsala{1 cup} & @Chicken Stock{2 cups}. Stir & bring the sauce to a gentle bubble.

Season the sauce with @Salt, @Black Pepper{}, & @Parsley{1/4 cup}.

Once the sauce thickens, return the pork to the skillet & turn to coat it in the sauce. Serve with the mashed potatoes.
