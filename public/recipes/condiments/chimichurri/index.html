Chimichurri

Condiments, Argentinian, Sauce

Ingredients

  1. Fresh Parsley — 1 cup
  2. Garlic — 3-4 cloves
  3. Fresh Oregano — 2 tbsp
  4. Dried Oregano — 2 tsp
  5. Extra Virgin Olive Oil — 1/2 cup
  6. Red Wine Vinegar — 2 tbsp
  7. Coarse Salt — 1 tsp
  8. Ground Black Pepper — 1/4 tsp
  9. Red Pepper Flakes — 1/4 tsp

Cookware

  1. small bowl
  2. measuring cup

Instructions

  1. Finely chop Fresh Parsley, Garlic, and Fresh Oregano (or use Dried Oregano).

  2. Place the chopped herbs and garlic into a small bowl.

  3. In a measuring cup, stir together Extra Virgin Olive Oil, Red Wine Vinegar, Coarse Salt, Ground Black Pepper, and Red Pepper Flakes.

  4. Pour the oil mixture over the parsley mixture and stir until well combined.

  5. Adjust seasoning to taste and serve immediately or refrigerate.
