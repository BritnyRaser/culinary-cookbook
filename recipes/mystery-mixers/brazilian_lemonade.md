Title: Brazilian Lemonade
Servings: 5 cups
Tags: Brazilian
Link: https://braziliankitchenabroad.com/brazilian-lemonade/
---

Slice @Limes{4} in quarters & add them to a #blender

Add the @Condensed Milk{1/2 cup} & the @Water{4 cups} to the blender along with the limes & blend for no more than 15 seconds (the more you blend, the bitterer the limeade will turn out)

Using a #fine mesh strainer{}, strain the juice from the blender into a #pitcher filled with ice

Slice the remaining @lime{1} in half, & sqeeze into the pitcher for an extra punch of lime flavor

Stir & serve right away!
