---
Title: Venison Stew
Tags: American, Wild Game, Venison
---

Season the diced @Venison{2 lbs} with:
- @Flour{}
- @Salt{}
- @Black Pepper{}
- @Lawry's{}

Brown the seasoned venison in a #fryer or in a pan on the #stove.

Transfer the browned venison to a #large pot{} and add @Water{4 cups} & @Beef Cube{1}.

Bring the mixture to a boil. Reduce the heat, cover, and simmer until the venison is tender.

Add @Onion{1}, @Celery{2}, @Carrots{3}, and @Potatoes{2}. Continue cooking until the vegetables are tender.

Stir in @Peas and cook until heated through.
