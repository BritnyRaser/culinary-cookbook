--- 
Title: Cole Slaw
Tag: Homemade, German
---

Shred @Green Cabbage{1} & a small @Onion{1} thinly in a #large bowl{}.

Whisk together @Olive Oil{1/2 cup}, @Sugar{1/2 cup}, & @Vinegar{3 Tablespoons}, then pour the mixture over the shredded vegetables.

Season with @Salt & @Black Pepper{} to taste.

Let it sit for about ~{1 hour} before serving.
