--- 
Title: Mac & Cheese
---

Noodles:
Boil @Noodles{1/2 lb} in #pot

Cheese:
In separate #pan, melt @Butter{2 Tablespoons}.

Add @Flour{2 Tablespoons} & whick until mixed.

Slowly add @Milk{1.5 cups} & wisk fpr 4-6 minutes

Add shredded @Cheese{8 oz} slowly while whisking on NO heat!

Add  @Garlic Powder{1/8 teaspoon}.  If seafood, also add @Old Bay{1/8 teaspoon} & @Creole{1/8 teaspoon}

Smoke 225 degrees for ~{30 minutes} with #Applewood or Pecan wood chunks{}
