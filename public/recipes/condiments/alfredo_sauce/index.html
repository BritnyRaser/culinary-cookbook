Alfredo Sauce

Ingredients

Directions

  1. Add Unsalted Butter, Heavy Whipping Cream, and Cream Cheese to a medium saucepan and whisk over medium heat until melted.

  2. Add Minced Garlic, Garlic Powder, Italian Seasoning, Salt, and Ground Black Pepper.

  3. Continue to whisk until smooth before gradually adding Freshly Grated Parmesan Cheese.

  4. Bring to a simmer and continue to whisk for map[index:0 type:timer] or until it thickens.

  5. Toss it with your favorite pasta and serve immediately.
