Fruity Pebbles Rice Krispy Treats

Fruity Pebbles Rice Krispy Treats

Munchies, Homemade, Marshmellows, Rice Krispies, Fruity Pebbles, Butter

Ingredients

  1. Butter
  2. Butter — 1/4 cup
  3. Marshmellows — 1 package
  4. Rice Krispies — 3 cups
  5. Fruity Pebbles — 3 cups

Cookware

  1. 9x13 pan
  2. large pot

Instructions

  1. Spread Butter into 9x13 pan (so treats won't stick)

  2. Melt Butter in a large pot.

  3. Turn burner off, melt Marshmellows into the butter, stirring constantly.

  4. Add Rice Krispies & Fruity Pebbles into the melted marshmellow mixture, stirring until well coated.

  5. Place in pan
