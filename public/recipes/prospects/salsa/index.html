Salsa

Salsa

Prospects, Mexican, Tomato

Ingredients

  1. Roma Tomatoes — 1 lb
  2. Canned Diced Tomatoes — 1 can (14.5 oz)
  3. Fresh Cilantro — 1/2 cup
  4. Green Onions — 1/4 cup
  5. Jalapeño — 1 medium
  6. Garlic — 1 clove
  7. Lime Juice — 2 tbsp
  8. Cumin — 1/2 tsp
  9. Salt — 1/2 tsp
  10. Sugar — 1/4 tsp
  11. Salt
  12. Lime Juice

Cookware

  1. food processor
  2. serving bowl

Instructions

  1. Pulse Roma Tomatoes (halved and seeded), Canned Diced Tomatoes, Fresh Cilantro, Green Onions, Jalapeño (seeded and chopped), Garlic, Lime Juice, Cumin, Salt, and Sugar in a food processor until desired consistency is reached.

  2. Taste and adjust Salt or Lime Juice as needed.

  3. Transfer to a serving bowl and serve with tortilla chips.
