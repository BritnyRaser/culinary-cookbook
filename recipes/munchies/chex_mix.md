---
Title: Chex Mix
Servings: 10 servings
Tags: Munchies, Homemade
---

Oven set at 250

Mix together @Wheat, Rice, Corn Chex Cereal{10 cups}, @Pretzels{2 cups}, @Peanuts{2 cups}, @Butter{6 Tablespoons}, @Worcestershire{5 Tablespoons}, @Lawry's Salt{1 1/2 teaspoons}, @Garlic Powder{3/4 cup} in #turkey oven bag{}

Place mixture onto #baking sheet{} and bake for ~{1 hour}, stir every 15 minutes.
