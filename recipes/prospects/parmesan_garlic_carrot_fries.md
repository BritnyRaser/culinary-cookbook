---
Title: Parmesan Garlic Carrot Fries
Tags: Prospects, Sidekicks
---

Set oven to 400F.

Slice @Carrots{2} into lengthwise fries length.

Mix Carrots with:
- @Oil{1 Tablespoon}
- @Parmesan Cheese{1/4 cup}
- @Garlic Powder{1 Tablespoon}
- @Black Pepper{}
- @Salt{}

Spread them onto a #baking sheet{}.

Bake for 15-20 minutes.

Serve topped with @Parmesan & @Parsley.

Optional Dip: @Greek Yogurt{2 Tablespoons}, @Lemon Juice{1 Tablespoon}, @Garlic Salt{1/2 teaspoon}, and @Black Pepper{1 teaspoon}.
