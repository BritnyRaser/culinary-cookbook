---
Title: Marry Me Chicken
Servings: 2-3 people
Tags: Italian-American
Link: https://gypsyplate.com/marry-me-chicken/
---

Mix all the chicken seasoning ingredients in a #small bowl{}:  @Paprika{1}, @Parsley{1 tsp}
@Salt{1/2 tsp}
@Pepper{1/2 tsp}

Season the @Chicken Thighs or Breasts{5-6 pieces} on both sides. 

Dredge the chicken in @Flour{1/4 cup} & shake off any excess.

In a #large skillet{}, melt the @Butter{2 Tablespoons} on medium heat. 

Brown the chicken, skin side first for ~{6-7 minutes} on each side, or until golden brown. Transfer onto a #plate & set aside.

In the same pan, add a little more butter if needed & sauté chopped @Shallot{1} for a minute. 

Add @Garlic{4 cloves} & cook for  ~{1 minute}, or until it's fragrant. 

Add @Red Chili Flakes{1 teaspoon}, @Thyme{1 teaspoon} & @Oregano{1 teaspoon} & mix well. 

Add in chopped @Sun Dried Tomatoes{1/3 cup} & cook for ~{2-3 minutes}.

Adjust the heat to medium-low, & add the @Heavy Cream{1 cup}, @Chicken Stock{1/2 cup} & the @Parmesan Cheese{3/4 cup} to the skillet. Mix & stir well till the cheese melts, & simmer it for ~{4-5 minutes}.

Mix in @Basil Leaves{1/4 cup} & place the chicken thighs in skin side up. Adjust the heat to medium low, cover, & cook till chicken is cooked through, stirring the sauce occasionally. Internal temperature for chicken thighs reaches 80-190°F & chicken breast cook till internal temperature reaches 165°F.

Garnish with some more basil leaves & serve immediately.
