Toasted Turkey Sandwich

Toasted Turkey Sandwich

Nom Nom Central, Poultry, Turkey, Bacon, Sandwich

Ingredients

  1. Oven-Roasted Turkey — 16 oz
  2. Bacon
  3. Red Peppers — 0.5
  4. Spinach
  5. Sharp Cheddar Jack Cheese
  6. Salt
  7. Pepper
  8. Fried Onion Cripsies
  9. Sauce

Instructions

  1. Lay out the Flatbread

  2. Layer on top on 1 side: Thin cut Oven-Roasted Turkey Optional to add Bacon. Sauted diced Red Peppers and Spinach Sharp Cheddar Jack Cheese

  3. Toast in Air Fryer at map[index:0 type:timer]

  4. Layer with: Salt and Pepper Fried Onion Cripsies Sauce
